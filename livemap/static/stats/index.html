What the rooftop has heard

One 1090 MHz antenna on a Tokyo roof picks up every aircraft that broadcasts within radio range. These are its totals since the first day of capture, and the size of the warehouse that keeps them. Aircraft on the FAA privacy list (LADD) are left out of every number, and no single airframe is ever named.
